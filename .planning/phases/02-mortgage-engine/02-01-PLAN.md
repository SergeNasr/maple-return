<feature>
  <name>Core Canadian Mortgage Amortization</name>
  <files>maple_return/mortgage.py, tests/test_mortgage.py</files>
  <behavior>
    The engine takes mortgage parameters and produces a list of monthly amortization entries.

    **Canadian semi-annual compounding convention:**
    - Nominal annual rate (e.g., 0.05 for 5%) is compounded semi-annually
    - Effective semi-annual rate = nominal_rate / 2
    - Effective monthly rate = (1 + nominal_rate / 2)^(1/6) - 1
    - This is the standard Canadian mortgage convention and MUST be implemented correctly

    **Input parameters (all using Decimal):**
    - purchase_price: Decimal (total property cost)
    - down_payment: Decimal (dollar amount, not percentage)
    - annual_rate: Decimal (nominal annual rate, e.g., Decimal("0.05") for 5%)
    - monthly_payment: Decimal (fixed payment amount per month)
    - amortization_months: int (total months, e.g., 360 for 30 years)
    - start_date: date (first payment date, derived from purchase date)

    **Output: list of AmortizationEntry dataclasses, each with:**
    - month_number: int (1-indexed)
    - date: date (month of this payment)
    - payment: Decimal (amount paid this month)
    - principal: Decimal (portion reducing the balance)
    - interest: Decimal (interest portion)
    - balance: Decimal (remaining balance after payment)

    **Rounding (Claude's discretion):**
    - Round interest to 2 decimal places each month
    - Principal = payment - interest (derived, not independently rounded)
    - Final month: if remaining balance < regular payment, payment = balance + interest for that month

    **Edge cases to test:**
    - Standard 30-year amortization at 5% rate
    - Payment exactly matching standard amortization payment (balance reaches 0 at month 360)
    - Payment higher than minimum (pays off early, fewer than amortization_months entries)
    - Payment lower than interest-only (balance grows -- should stop at amortization_months and report remaining balance)
    - First month: full interest on initial principal
    - Final month: adjusted payment to zero out balance exactly

    **Also create a helper function:**
    - `calculate_monthly_rate(annual_rate: Decimal) -> Decimal` -- converts nominal annual rate to effective monthly rate using Canadian semi-annual compounding
    - `calculate_standard_payment(principal: Decimal, monthly_rate: Decimal, num_months: int) -> Decimal` -- standard amortization payment formula (needed for renewal payment recalculation in Plan 02-02)
  </behavior>
  <implementation>
    Create `maple_return/mortgage.py` with:
    1. `AmortizationEntry` dataclass for monthly schedule rows
    2. `calculate_monthly_rate()` helper for Canadian semi-annual compounding
    3. `calculate_standard_payment()` helper for standard mortgage payment formula: P * r * (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)
    4. `calculate_amortization()` main function that iterates month by month:
       - Each month: interest = balance * monthly_rate (rounded to 2 dp), principal = payment - interest, new_balance = balance - principal
       - Stop when balance <= 0 or month_number > amortization_months
       - Final month adjustment: payment = balance + interest (exact payoff)

    Use Decimal throughout (per project convention from Phase 1). Use `decimal.ROUND_HALF_UP` for rounding to 2 places.
  </implementation>
</feature>
