**Data structures** (in `maple_return/operating.py`):

Create an `OperatingInput` dataclass:
- `monthly_rent`: Decimal (base monthly rent in CAD)
- `vacancy_rate`: Decimal (0.0 to 1.0, e.g., 0.05 for 5%)
- `property_tax_annual`: Decimal (annual amount in CAD)
- `insurance_annual`: Decimal (annual amount in CAD)
- `maintenance_annual`: Decimal (annual amount in CAD)
- `management_fee_rate`: Decimal (% of gross rent, e.g., 0.10 for 10%)
- `rent_escalation_rate`: Decimal (annual %, e.g., 0.02 for 2%)
- `expense_escalation_rate`: Decimal (annual %, e.g., 0.02 for 2%)
- `lease_start_date`: date (for lease anniversary escalation timing)

Create a `MonthlyOperating` dataclass:
- `month`: date
- `gross_rent`: Decimal (rent before vacancy)
- `vacancy_deduction`: Decimal (gross_rent * vacancy_rate)
- `effective_rent`: Decimal (gross_rent - vacancy_deduction)
- `property_tax`: Decimal (monthly portion)
- `insurance`: Decimal (monthly portion)
- `maintenance`: Decimal (monthly portion)
- `management_fee`: Decimal (management_fee_rate * gross_rent)
- `total_expenses`: Decimal (sum of all expense line items)
- `noi`: Decimal (effective_rent - total_expenses, Net Operating Income)

**Functions to implement:**

`calculate_monthly_operating(input_data: OperatingInput, month: date) -> MonthlyOperating`
- Calculate rent for this month:
  - Determine how many full years have passed since lease_start_date (floor division of months elapsed / 12)
  - Apply compounding escalation: `base_rent * (1 + escalation_rate) ^ years_elapsed`
  - This means rent increases on the lease anniversary date (per locked decision)
- Apply vacancy: `vacancy_deduction = gross_rent * vacancy_rate` (evenly across all months per locked decision)
- Calculate expenses for this month:
  - Years elapsed from first month of operation (use calendar year for expense escalation — Claude's discretion)
  - Actually, use the same lease_start_date for expense escalation timing for consistency
  - Base monthly = annual / 12 for property_tax, insurance, maintenance
  - Apply escalation: `base_monthly * (1 + expense_escalation_rate) ^ years_elapsed`
  - Management fee = `management_fee_rate * gross_rent` (scales with income, NOT vacancy-adjusted, per locked decision that it's % of gross rent)
- Calculate NOI = effective_rent - total_expenses
- Use Decimal throughout, quantize monetary results to 2 decimal places (ROUND_HALF_UP)

`generate_operating_schedule(input_data: OperatingInput, start_date: date, num_months: int) -> list[MonthlyOperating]`
- Generate a list of MonthlyOperating entries for consecutive months
- Use the `_add_months` helper from mortgage.py for date progression (import it)
- This produces the full operating schedule that Plan 02 will combine with mortgage data
